HOW TO READ THIS

VERIFICATION & IMPORTANT ASSUMPTIONS

The overhead motor is removed throughout scanning. Other center stickers are hidden, so the cube needs a known center-color mapping / keyed insertion orientation. Camera readings here are perfect simulated colors; real OpenCV detection will need confidence checks and rescans.

Simultaneous opposite turns are geometrically valid, but must be confirmed mechanically. If your mechanism cannot safely turn both at once, run them sequentially.
